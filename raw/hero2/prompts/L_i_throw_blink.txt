Pixel art sprite sheet for a 2D side-scrolling action game, animated like classic 2D arcade fighting game sprites: dynamic, exaggerated, full of weight and momentum. Use the character in the reference images exactly: white shoulder-length straight hair, wine-red fitted long coat with gold buttons and gold trim, black trousers and tall black boots, white bandages on the right forearm with a gold ring at the wrist, and a huge broad greatsword with a flat square tip, dark steel blade with a gold engraved line.

Layout: a 4 columns x 3 rows grid of 12 equal cells on a solid pure green (#00FF00) background, no grid lines, no text, no numbers. Read left to right, top to bottom as frames 1 to 12. Row 1 and row 2 are one continuous skill: hurling the greatsword forward like a spear. Row 3 is a separate instant-teleport arrival followed by a rising slash.

Row 1, the throw:
1. Ready stance, sword in the right hand.
2. Drawing the sword back over the right shoulder, both hands on the hilt, body coiled and turned slightly.
3. Hurling it forward: the arm whipping over and forward, body uncoiling.
4. Arm fully extended forward, fingers just opening, the hand empty, body rotated through the throw.

Row 2, follow-through and recovery:
5. Follow-through: torso twisted away, empty right hand swept down across the body.
6. Snapping the body back upright, empty hand, coat swinging past.
7. Low ready stance, empty right hand raised in front like a guide, left hand open behind.
8. Coiled forward, empty right hand reaching out, knees bent, ready to dash.

Row 3, instantaneous arrival and the slash:
9. Crouched very low facing right, the greatsword suddenly back in the right hand, pulled back at the hip against the ground.
10. Explosive rising diagonal slash: body uncoiling upward, blade sweeping from low right hip to high in front, arm extended.
11. Follow-through of the slash: body turned, blade swept down and out in front at knee height.
12. Settled ready stance, sword at guard, identical to frame 1.

Every frame: the SAME character, strict side view facing RIGHT (the body may twist, but the head and feet keep facing right; never face the camera, never turn away); identical character scale in every cell; feet on the same ground line in every cell; whole body inside the cell with margin, the whole sword inside the cell in every frame where it is held (frames 4 to 8 have no sword). Crisp pixel art, limited palette of about 24 colors, hard-edged pixels, no anti-aliasing, no slash effects, no motion trails, no afterimages, no dust (effects are added in code), no background objects, no extra characters, no floating swords, no text.
