Pixel art sprite sheet for a 2D side-scrolling action game, animated like classic 2D arcade fighting game sprites: dynamic, exaggerated, full of weight and momentum. Use the character in the reference image exactly: white shoulder-length straight hair, wine-red fitted long coat with gold buttons and gold trim, black trousers and tall black boots, white bandages on the right forearm with a gold ring at the wrist, and a huge broad greatsword with a flat square tip, dark steel blade with a gold engraved line.

Layout: a 4 columns x 3 rows grid of twelve equal cells on a solid pure green (#00FF00) background, no grid lines, no text, no numbers. Read left to right, top to bottom as frames 1 to 12 of ONE continuous three-hit sword combo. Each hit flows directly into the next: the last pose of a hit is the wind-up of the next hit, the character never returns to a neutral standing pose in the middle.

Row 3, hit three, rising launcher slash:

Every frame: the SAME character, strict side view facing RIGHT (the body may twist, but the head and feet keep facing right; never face the camera, never turn away); identical character scale in every cell; feet near the same baseline; whole body and whole sword inside the cell with margin. Crisp pixel art, limited palette of about 24 colors, hard-edged pixels, no anti-aliasing, no slash effects or motion trails (they will be added in code), no background objects, no extra characters, no gravestones.
