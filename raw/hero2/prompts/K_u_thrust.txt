Pixel art sprite sheet for a 2D side-scrolling action game, animated like classic 2D arcade fighting game sprites: dynamic, exaggerated, full of weight and momentum. Use the character in the reference images exactly: white shoulder-length straight hair, wine-red fitted long coat with gold buttons and gold trim, black trousers and tall black boots, white bandages on the right forearm with a gold ring at the wrist, and a huge broad greatsword with a flat square tip, dark steel blade with a gold engraved line.

Layout: a 4 columns x 2 rows grid of 8 equal cells on a solid pure green (#00FF00) background, no grid lines, no text, no numbers. Read left to right, top to bottom as frames 1 to 8. All eight frames are ONE continuous skill: a low explosive forward thrust that drives the whole body about one body-length to the right, then a sharp braking recovery.

1. Ready stance, both hands on the hilt, sword angled low in front.
2. Coiling back and down, weight shifting onto the rear leg, the sword pulled back beside the hip, point aimed straight forward to the right.
3. Deepest crouch, body compressed like a spring, coat tails gathered, blade still aimed forward.
4. Launch: rear leg driving, body stretching forward and low, blade starting to shoot forward.
5. Peak of the thrust: body stretched horizontally and very low, blade thrust straight forward at waist height with the arm fully extended, rear leg trailing far behind, coat streaming straight back.
6. Passing through: same low stretched pose, blade still forward, coat and hair whipping back.
7. Braking: front foot planted hard, body rising and leaning back against the momentum, blade sweeping out to the right side.
8. Recovered ready stance, sword back at guard, identical to frame 1.

Every frame: the SAME character, strict side view facing RIGHT (the body may twist, but the head and feet keep facing right; never face the camera, never turn away); identical character scale in every cell; feet on the same ground line in every cell; whole body and whole sword inside the cell with margin. Crisp pixel art, limited palette of about 24 colors, hard-edged pixels, no anti-aliasing, no slash effects, no motion trails, no afterimages, no dust (effects are added in code), no background objects, no extra characters, no text.
