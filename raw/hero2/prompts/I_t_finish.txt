Pixel art sprite sheet for a 2D side-scrolling action game, animated like classic 2D arcade fighting game sprites: dynamic, exaggerated, full of weight and momentum. Image 1 is the character's TRANSFORMED form, image 2 is his normal form. Unless a frame says otherwise, draw the TRANSFORMED form from image 1 exactly: white shoulder-length hair, glowing crimson eyes, wine-red long coat with gold buttons and trim, black trousers and boots, a hugely swollen crimson right arm with dark cracks ending in a giant claw, and a giant slab-like greatsword with a flat square tip and blood-red glowing engraved runes, gripped by the claw. It is heavy and slow, every move has huge weight.

Layout: a 4 columns x 2 rows grid of 8 equal cells on a solid pure green (#00FF00) background, no grid lines, no text, no numbers. Read left to right, top to bottom as frames 1 to 8. Row 1 is the jumping finisher, row 2 is the planted blade guard.

Row 1, jumping finisher slam:
1. Crouching low, blade gripped in both hands.  2. Leaping high, giant blade raised above the head, airborne.  3. Impact: crashing down, blade smashed into the ground with full force, deep crouch.  4. Rising slowly from the crouch, blade still in the ground.

Row 2, planted blade guard (he plants the giant blade upright in front of him as a wall):
5. Swinging the blade around to the front.  6. Slamming the blade upright into the ground in front as a wall, broad side facing right.  7. Braced behind the planted blade, shoulder and claw pressed against it, knees bent.  8. Same braced pose, straining.

Every frame: the SAME character, strict side view facing RIGHT (the body may twist, but the head and feet keep facing right; never face the camera, never turn away); identical character scale in every cell; within one row, feet on the same ground line unless the frame is airborne, in which case keep the real jump height inside the cell; whole body and whole sword inside the cell with margin. Crisp pixel art, limited palette of about 24 colors, hard-edged pixels, no anti-aliasing, no slash effects, no motion trails, no chains, no dust (effects are added in code), no background objects, no extra characters, no gravestones, no text.
