Every frame: strict side view facing RIGHT; identical scale in every cell; whole subject inside its cell with margin. Crisp pixel art in the same style and palette as the reference image (dark gothic, wine red, crimson, black, bone grey, small gold accents), limited palette of about 24 colors, hard-edged pixels, no anti-aliasing, no soft glow gradients, no grid lines, no text, no numbers, no background objects, no ground, no hero character.
