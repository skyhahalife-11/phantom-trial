Pixel art sprite sheet for a 2D side-scrolling action game. Subject: the finishing move "Burial": a huge spectral crimson reaper: a hooded wraith with no visible face except two burning crimson eyes inside a black hood, a tattered wine-red cloak with gold-trimmed edges echoing the hero's coat, two long bony arms ending in giant crimson claws with dark cracks, and NO legs: its lower body dissolves into broken black tombstone shards, rusted chains and wisps of crimson smoke. It lifts a COLOSSAL gothic tombstone with both claws and smashes it down onto the ground. The tombstone is black cracked stone with glowing blood-red runes, taller than the reaper. Keep the same reaper design and scale in every frame; the bottom of each cell is the ground line where the tombstone lands.

Layout: a 4 columns x 2 rows grid of 8 equal cells. Read left to right, top to bottom as frames 1 to 8.
Row 1: 1. the reaper rising, a colossal tombstone materializing from crimson smoke above its claws. 2. holding the colossal tombstone high overhead with both claws, runes blazing. 3. leaning back, tombstone at the peak, about to swing. 4. swinging the tombstone down in front of it.
Row 2: 5. impact: the tombstone slammed into the ground at the bottom right, a huge burst of red cracks, rubble and dust. 6. the tombstone breaking apart, shockwave spreading. 7. the reaper dissolving into smoke and shards, rubble everywhere. 8. only falling rubble and fading crimson smoke.

Every frame: strict side view facing RIGHT; identical scale in every cell; whole subject inside its cell with margin. Crisp pixel art in the same style and palette as the reference image (dark gothic, wine red, crimson, black, bone grey, small gold accents), limited palette of about 24 colors, hard-edged pixels, no anti-aliasing, no soft glow gradients, no grid lines, no text, no numbers, no background objects, no ground, no hero character. Background: solid pure green (#00FF00) everywhere outside the subject.
