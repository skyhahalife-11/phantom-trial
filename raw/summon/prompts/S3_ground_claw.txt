Pixel art sprite sheet for a 2D side-scrolling action game. Subject: a gigantic crimson demonic claw summoned from beneath the ground, the same claw design as the hero's transformed right arm in the reference image (swollen crimson flesh, dark cracks, glowing red veins, long black-tipped talons), but much bigger: an arm bursting up out of a crack in the ground. The bottom of each cell is the ground line; the arm always comes out of the ground at the bottom center.

Layout: a 4 columns x 3 rows grid of 12 equal cells. Read left to right, top to bottom as frames 1 to 12.
Row 1, emerge and grab: 1. ground cracking open with red light and rubble. 2. the claw erupting upward, fingers spread, rubble flying. 3. claw high and open, reaching forward. 4. claw closed into a tight fist at the top (as if gripping an enemy).
Row 2, slam: 5. fist raised high and pulled back. 6. fist swinging down in an arc. 7. impact: fist smashed onto the ground to the right, shockwave of red cracks and rubble. 8. fist lifting up again.
Row 3, toss and retreat: 9. arm swinging forward and up. 10. claw opening at the top, flinging (empty hand, the enemy is drawn separately). 11. arm sinking back into the ground. 12. only the glowing crack in the ground closing, a few pebbles.

Every frame: strict side view facing RIGHT; identical scale in every cell; whole subject inside its cell with margin. Crisp pixel art in the same style and palette as the reference image (dark gothic, wine red, crimson, black, bone grey, small gold accents), limited palette of about 24 colors, hard-edged pixels, no anti-aliasing, no soft glow gradients, no grid lines, no text, no numbers, no background objects, no ground, no hero character. Background: solid pure green (#00FF00) everywhere outside the subject.
