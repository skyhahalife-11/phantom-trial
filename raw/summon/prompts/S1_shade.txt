Pixel art sprite sheet for a 2D side-scrolling action game. Subject: the "Blood Pact Shade", summoned behind the hero from the reference image when he transforms (like a ghost demon that floats behind a demon swordsman). It is a huge spectral crimson reaper: a hooded wraith with no visible face except two burning crimson eyes inside a black hood, a tattered wine-red cloak with gold-trimmed edges echoing the hero's coat, two long bony arms ending in giant crimson claws with dark cracks, and NO legs: its lower body dissolves into broken black tombstone shards, rusted chains and wisps of crimson smoke. Semi-translucent ghostly feel shown with dithered pixel edges, menacing and elegant.

Layout: a 4 columns x 3 rows grid of 12 equal cells. Read left to right, top to bottom as frames 1 to 12.
Row 1, floating idle loop: 1-4. hovering, cloak and chains swaying, claws slowly flexing, slight bob up and down.
Row 2, claw swipe (it mirrors the hero's basic attack): 5. right claw drawn back high. 6. claw slashing forward and down in a huge arc, body leaning in. 7. claw fully extended forward low, fingers spread. 8. recovering back to hover.
Row 3, summon and dismiss: 9. only a swirl of crimson smoke and shards. 10. half-formed shade rising out of the smoke. 11. fully formed, both claws spread wide, roaring. 12. bursting apart into tombstone shards and smoke.

Every frame: strict side view facing RIGHT; identical scale in every cell; whole subject inside its cell with margin. Crisp pixel art in the same style and palette as the reference image (dark gothic, wine red, crimson, black, bone grey, small gold accents), limited palette of about 24 colors, hard-edged pixels, no anti-aliasing, no soft glow gradients, no grid lines, no text, no numbers, no background objects, no ground, no hero character. Background: solid pure green (#00FF00) everywhere outside the subject.
